Privacy Policy for MyAgent

Last updated: October 8, 2026

MyAgent is an application designed to help businesses manage Instagram messages, comments, and related content through Meta's official APIs.

1. Information We Collect

MyAgent may process information provided through Instagram and Meta APIs, including Instagram account information, comments, messages, usernames, and other information necessary to provide the application's features.

2. How We Use Information

The information is used only to provide and operate MyAgent's Instagram automation features, including responding to comments and sending messages when authorized by the Instagram account owner.

3. Data Sharing

MyAgent does not sell personal information. Information obtained through Meta APIs is not shared with third parties except where necessary to provide the requested service or comply with applicable law.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the application against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

5. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as necessary to provide the application's functionality or as required by applicable law.

6. User Rights

Users may request information about their data or request deletion of data associated with the application, subject to applicable legal requirements.
